Academy Sports + Outdoors — Sponsor LBO

A hypothetical five-year sponsor take-private model focused on entry valuation, leverage, debt paydown, operating growth, and exit returns.

6.37xEntry Multiple
4.5xOpening Debt
2.86x5-Year MOIC
23.4%5-Year IRR

Problem

Test whether a sponsor could generate attractive returns under a defined operating case without relying on multiple expansion.

Approach

Built sources & uses, operating projections, debt schedules, cash sweep, exit valuation, and IRR/MOIC sensitivities across leverage and exit multiple assumptions.

Key assumptions

  • 25% offer premium to the project reference price.
  • 4.5x opening debt/EBITDA.
  • 5-year hold and 6.0x exit multiple.
  • Revenue growth around 3.5%–4.0% with modest margin expansion.

Results & interpretation

  • Sponsor equity is approximately $1.63B.
  • Debt paydown is approximately $2.22B.
  • Exit equity value is approximately $4.67B.
  • Base-case returns are approximately 2.86x MOIC and 23.4% IRR.

Risks / limitations

  • Consumer demand and discretionary spending.
  • Margin execution.
  • Financing cost and refinancing risk.
  • Exit multiple compression.

What I learned

I learned to separate return creation into EBITDA growth, deleveraging, and exit valuation rather than treating IRR as a black-box output.
