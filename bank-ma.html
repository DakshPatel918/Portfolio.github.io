JPMorgan Chase × Fifth Third — Hypothetical Bank M&A

A bank-specific accretion/dilution model testing purchase accounting, tangible book value, capital impact, and synergies in a hypothetical all-stock transaction.

Problem

Evaluate whether a hypothetical bank acquisition can be EPS-accretive while also understanding tangible-book and regulatory-capital consequences.

Approach

Built deal mechanics, new-share issuance, purchase accounting, credit mark, core deposit intangible, synergy phase-in, EPS accretion/dilution, TBVPS, and CET1 impact.

Key assumptions

  • 100% stock consideration.
  • 15% target expense cost saves and 2% revenue synergies.
  • 50% synergy realization in Year 1 and 100% in Year 2.
  • Illustrative purchase-accounting marks.

Results & interpretation

  • Year 1 EPS is roughly flat/slightly accretive.
  • Year 2 EPS accretion is approximately 0.9%.
  • Pro forma TBVPS dilution is approximately 14.5%.
  • Illustrative pro forma CET1 is approximately 13.75%.

Risks / limitations

  • The transaction is intentionally hypothetical and may face substantial regulatory/deposit-concentration constraints.
  • Purchase-accounting assumptions are illustrative.
  • Synergy realization can differ materially from assumptions.

What I learned

I learned why bank M&A cannot be judged on EPS accretion alone; tangible book value, capital, credit marks, and regulation are equally important.
