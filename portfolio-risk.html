Problem

Move beyond return alone by measuring volatility, beta, drawdown, tail risk, concentration, and diversification in one portfolio view.

Approach

Constructed a model portfolio, calculated annualized return/volatility, Sharpe, beta, VaR/CVaR, maximum drawdown, position/sector concentration, and effective holdings.

Key assumptions

  • 10-stock model portfolio with SPY benchmark.
  • 4.0% risk-free rate in the project.
  • 60-month deterministic illustrative return series.

Results & interpretation

  • 1-month 95% VaR is approximately 8.2%.
  • CVaR is approximately 9.3%.
  • Largest position is 14%; technology exposure is 39%.
  • Effective holdings are approximately 9.5.

Risks / limitations

  • Return data are illustrative rather than realized history.
  • VaR is model-dependent and does not capture all tail behavior.
  • Concentration and correlations can change rapidly.

What I learned

I learned to present portfolio risk as a collection of complementary measures rather than relying on volatility or Sharpe alone.
