Residential Property Investment & Renovation

A real-world self-directed project combining acquisition screening, renovation budgeting, capital allocation, project execution, and resale economics.

Problem

Identify an undervalued residential property, allocate renovation capital efficiently, manage execution risk, and improve the economics of a resale.

Approach

Conducted market analysis, developed a renovation budget and financial forecast, managed materials procurement and renovation execution, and participated in resale negotiations.

Key assumptions

  • The public case study uses only the financial figures already disclosed on the résumé.
  • Property address, counterparties, and other private transaction details are intentionally omitted.

Results & interpretation

  • Résumé reports a $20K materials budget.
  • Résumé reports approximately $40K profit and 50% ROI within four months.
  • Project was completed on schedule and within budget according to the résumé.

Risks / limitations

  • Real-estate returns depend on acquisition basis, renovation scope, financing, taxes, transaction costs, and market conditions.
  • The public portfolio intentionally omits private property-level documentation.

What I learned

This project gave me direct experience connecting an investment thesis to capital allocation and execution, rather than analyzing a transaction only on paper.
