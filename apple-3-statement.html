Apple — Full 3-Statement Financial Model

An integrated historical and forecast model linking the income statement, balance sheet, and cash flow statement through operating and capital-allocation assumptions.

$633.6B2030E Revenue

$13.812030E EPS

Problem

Build a forecast in which operating assumptions flow consistently through all three statements and balance/cash checks reconcile.

Approach

Loaded 2023A–2025A historicals, forecast revenue and margins through 2030E, linked working capital, D&A, capex, debt, dividends, repurchases, and ending cash, then used automated balance-sheet and cash checks.

Key assumptions

  • Revenue growth moderates from 13% in 2026E toward 6% in 2030E.
  • Gross margin remains near the high-40% range.
  • Capital returns are modeled through dividends and repurchases.

Results & interpretation

  • Revenue reaches approximately $633.6B in 2030E.
  • Net income reaches approximately $185.8B in 2030E.
  • Diluted EPS reaches approximately $13.81 in 2030E.
  • Free cash flow reaches approximately $210.7B in 2030E.

Risks / limitations

  • Forecasts depend heavily on growth and margin assumptions.
  • Capital-return assumptions can materially change ending cash and share count.
  • This is an independent educational model, not company guidance.

What I learned

The project reinforced how one assumption can affect several statements at once, especially working capital, capital allocation, share count, and cash reconciliation.
