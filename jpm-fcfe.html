JPMorgan Chase — FCFE Valuation

A bank-specific equity valuation using FCFE rather than a conventional FCFF/WACC framework.

Problem

Value a large bank while respecting the fact that deposits and debt are part of the operating model, making a standard industrial-company DCF less appropriate.

Approach

Forecast net income, estimate the equity capital required to support growth, treat the residual as distributable FCFE, discount it at the cost of equity, and calculate a Gordon-growth terminal value with sensitivity analysis.

Key assumptions

  • Cost of equity: 9.5%.
  • Terminal growth: 3.0%.
  • Forecast period: 2026E–2030E.

Results & interpretation

  • Base-case implied value is approximately $292.40/share.
  • The result sits below the project reference price, making sustainable premium profitability a key debate.
  • Sensitivity analysis shows meaningful dependence on cost of equity and terminal growth.

Risks / limitations

  • NII and interest-rate sensitivity.
  • Credit normalization and charge-offs.
  • Regulatory/capital requirements.
  • Terminal-value sensitivity.

What I learned

I learned why financial institutions require a different valuation mindset and how retained equity capital connects growth, ROE, and distributable cash flow.
